Experiments to try
  1. Increase the surface size with a centered charge. The field weakens, but flux stays constant.
  2. Move the charge off-center. Compare the uneven colors with the total flux.
  3. Move the charge outside. Inward and outward contributions cancel.
  4. Enable the neutral pair. Zero net flux can coexist with a nonzero field.
  5. Change the shape while keeping the charges enclosed.
  6. Increase resolution near a crossing to explore quadrature accuracy.
